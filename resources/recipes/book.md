## Grandma's Lasagna

- **Servings:** 6
- **Source:** Family recipe
- **Notes:** Layer: sauce → noodles → ricotta mix → meat → mozzarella. Repeat 3x. Top with extra mozzarella. Bake 375°F covered 45min, uncovered 15min.

### Ingredients

| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| lasagna noodles | 1 box (16 oz) |
| ground beef 80/20 | 1.5 lbs |
| Italian pork sausage | 0.5 lb |
| whole milk ricotta | 32 oz |
| shredded mozzarella | 3 cups |
| Parmesan | 1 cup grated |
| large eggs | 2 |
| crushed tomatoes | 28 oz can |
| tomato paste | 6 oz can |
| yellow onion | 1 medium |
| fresh garlic | 4 cloves |
| fresh basil | 1 bunch |
| Italian parsley | 1 bunch |
| dried oregano | pantry |
| olive oil | pantry |
